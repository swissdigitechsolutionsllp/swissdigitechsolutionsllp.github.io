FAQ

Questions worth answering before we start.

A quick introduction to how Swiss DigiTech Solutions LLP approaches projects, services and collaboration.

Do you work with businesses outside Mohali and India?

Yes. Our service model supports remote collaboration and businesses with national or international audiences.

Can I choose only one service?

Yes. You can engage us for a focused requirement such as a website, SEO or a paid campaign, or combine services when a connected approach makes more sense.

How do you begin a project?

We start with a conversation about your goals, current position, audience, requirements and timeline before recommending a suitable scope.

Do you provide custom website and application development?

Yes. Development work can be planned around your operational needs, user requirements and future growth.

How will we communicate during the project?

We agree on practical communication points and keep you informed about progress, decisions and next steps.

How do I request a proposal?

Use the contact page to share your requirements. We can then discuss the project and shape an appropriate proposal.
