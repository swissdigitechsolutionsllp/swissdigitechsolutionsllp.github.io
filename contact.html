Frequently Asked

Questions we hear before a project starts.

What services does Swiss DigiTech Solutions LLP offer?

A complete range of digital services: website design and development, custom web applications, iOS and Android mobile app development, SEO, pay-per-click advertising, social media marketing, branding and creative design, and email marketing.

Where is Swiss DigiTech Solutions LLP based?

We're a global technology agency with teams in Switzerland and India, headquartered at Vista Tower in Mohali, Punjab. Our dual presence lets us support clients across different time zones without disruption.

Do you work with small businesses, or only large companies?

Both. Whether you're a startup just getting started or an established company expanding internationally, we tailor solutions to fit your goals and budget.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A standard website is usually delivered within a few weeks; larger projects like custom applications or mobile apps can take longer. We always agree timelines clearly before work begins.

Are your services affordable for growing businesses?

Yes. Quality digital services shouldn't be limited to big budgets. Our pricing is competitive and our solutions are built to scale, so you only pay for what your business actually needs.

How do I get started?

Fill in the form on this page or reach out on any of our social channels. Share a little about your project, and we'll come back with the right plan to move forward.
